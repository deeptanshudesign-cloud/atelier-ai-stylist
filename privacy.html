What we collect

  • Account: your email address and a salted hash of your password (we never store the password itself).
  • Style profile: what you choose to enter, such as name, country, height, sizes, body type, budget, climate, styles you like and things to avoid.
  • Wardrobe and looks: the clothing items you add or confirm after a closet scan, and the outfits we generate for you, including those you save.
  • Technical data: your IP address is used briefly for abuse prevention and rate limiting, and server logs may record errors.

Photos

If you use closet scanning or camera try-on, the photo is sent from your browser to our server and on to our AI provider to be analysed. We do not save these photos. They are processed in memory and discarded when the analysis finishes. Only the clothing items you choose to add, or the text feedback we show you, are kept. Camera access happens only after you tap the button and grant your browser's permission; you can revoke it at any time.

How we use your data

  • To create outfits, scan your closet, check your fit and look up live product listings.
  • To run your account, sign you in and send password-reset emails.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

Who we share it with

  • AI provider (Anthropic): receives your style profile, wardrobe item names, your request, and any photo you submit, so it can generate results. See Anthropic's privacy policy for how it handles data.
  • Email provider ([EMAIL PROVIDER]): receives your email address to deliver reset emails.
  • Hosting and database provider ([HOST NAME]): stores the data described above on our behalf.
  • Retailers: if you tap "Buy now" or "Find it", you leave Atelier and the retailer's own policies apply. We don't pass your profile to them.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

Cookies and local storage

We use no tracking or advertising cookies. Your browser's local storage keeps a sign-in token so you stay logged in. Signing out removes it.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Password-reset tokens expire after one hour. When you delete your account, your profile, wardrobe, looks and sign-in details are deleted immediately from our database. Backups may retain copies for up to [BACKUP DAYS] days.
